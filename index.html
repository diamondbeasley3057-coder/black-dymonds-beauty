Meet the Founder

The Visionary
Behind Black Dymonds

Black Dymonds Beauty began with one chair, one client, and an unwavering belief that beauty service should feel like luxury — every single time. What started as a single stylist's craft has grown into a full-service studio rooted in artistry, excellence, and community.

The next chapter is bigger. The vision is to open a dedicated salon suite in Cleveland — a space built not just for one stylist, but for an entire community of beauty professionals to grow together under one roof.

"This isn't just about a chair or a suite. It's about building a brand — a home where stylists, makeup artists, and beauty entrepreneurs can build their own success stories alongside mine."
